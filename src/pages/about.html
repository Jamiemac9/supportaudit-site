In short

Who is behind SEA Audit?

SEA Audit is an independent audit service for supported housing in England, founded by Jamie McCollin. It combines hands-on experience of auditing resident support files with a structured audit system that checks every file the same way, and an auditor who reads every file, makes every judgement and sets every grade.

What we stand for

Four principles in every audit.

Evidence first

Every finding names the document and the date. If something isn't in the file, we record it as not seen. Nothing is assumed and nothing is invented.

Constructive

The point is improvement. Every issue comes with an example of what good looks like, and strengths are named as specifically as gaps.

A person decides

Our system checks. An experienced auditor reads the whole file, sets every grade and explains the reason for it.

Confidential

Resident records are special category data. We handle them that way from transfer to deletion.

Our method

Consistent checks. Human judgement.

A structured audit system makes every check consistent and thorough. An experienced auditor makes it fair. Here is exactly who does what.

What our audit system checks

  • Checks every file against the same checklist
  • Flags missing signatures, dates and documents
  • Calculates review due dates and weekly support hours
  • Matches session notes to support goals
  • Brings the evidence together for the auditor

What the auditor does

  • Reviews every flag and every judgement
  • Reads the file as a whole, in context
  • Sets the final grade and writes the reason
  • Names the strengths and the next steps
  • Talks it through with your team
How we write

Plain, specific and fair.

Our reports are written to be used by support workers, not filed by managers.

  • UK English, written the way an experienced auditor speaks
  • Every issue names the document, the date and what is wrong
  • Every issue includes a short example of what good looks like
  • Strengths drawn from the documents, never generic praise
  • Gaps stated plainly: "No session notes evidence work on this goal"
Data protection

How we handle resident data.

Resident support files contain special category data under UK GDPR. These commitments apply to every audit.

01

Agreed before anything is shared

A data processing agreement and retention period are agreed with you before any files are sent.

02

Only what is needed

We request only the files and the review period in scope, and nothing else.

03

Secure transfer and storage

Files are shared through a secure transfer and stored with access limited to the audit team.

04

Deleted on schedule

Resident data is deleted at the end of the agreed retention period, and we confirm it in writing.

05

Not used to train AI

Resident data is used only to deliver your audit. It is never used to train AI models.

Registered
