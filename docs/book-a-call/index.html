About the call

What to expect.

Is the discovery call free?

Yes. There is no charge and no obligation. If an audit isn't the right next step for you, we will say so.

Who will I speak to?

An auditor, not a salesperson. The person you speak to understands supported housing files and can answer detailed questions.

What should I prepare?

Nothing formal. It helps to know roughly how many residents and managing agents are involved, and what prompted you to get in touch.

Should I send resident files before the call?

No. Resident data is only shared once a data processing agreement is in place. Please don't include resident details in the form.
